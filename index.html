FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Flowly replace my calendar?

No. It works on top of Google Calendar or Outlook and writes your plan back into it.

What happens after the free trial?

You stay on the free Starter plan unless you choose to upgrade. Nothing is charged automatically.

Can I use it with my team?

Yes. The Team plan adds shared views, async standups and admin controls.

Is my data safe?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we never sell or share it.
